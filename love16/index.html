彼のトリセツ

Possible reasons, and a message worth sending, by his 16 types.

彼のトリセツ (Kare no Torisetsu, “his manual”) is a reading app for the nights you keep checking your phone. Pick your type and his, and read the reasons that are possible right now and a message you could send. The app and its screens are in Japanese only, so this page describes them in English.

Coming soon on the App Store iPhone · Japanese only · Free to start

His situation

Why he may have changed, and what to send.

For situations like a sudden cooling-off, being left on read, signs of interest, after an argument, or less contact, the app lists three possible reasons for his type, how to tell them apart, an example message you can send, and one you had better not.

  • A “check first” list helps you judge whether he really fits the type.
  • The figures are rough guides, not a verdict on his feelings.

Compatibility

Any pair of the 16 types.

Choose your type and his, and the result appears right away: why you are drawn together, where you tend to miss each other, and one tip. The basics are free for all 256 pairs, and the result can be shared as an image without his name.

  • The score is a rough guide, not a ranking of good and bad.

Today’s question

One question to ask him each day.

Each day the app gives one question to ask him and a message you can send as it is. Record his reaction, or just record it without sending. A morning reminder is optional.

  • The question is free every day. Up to 7 records are free.

His profile book

64 items, filled in a little each day.

Recorded answers slowly fill his profile book, 64 items in all.

  • The talk check is free 3 times: paste a message before you send it and see how his type may take it, with a gentler rewording. It runs on your iPhone.
  • A daily ranking lines up the 16 types by a different theme each day.

Questions

Is there an English version?

No. The app and its content are in Japanese only, and the first release is planned for Japan. The privacy and support pages linked here are also in Japanese.

Is this an official personality test?

No. It is an unofficial reading based on 16 types, and it has no connection with any official assessment body or test. The text describes tendencies, not what he feels or how things will turn out.

Where is my data kept?

On your iPhone. There is no account and no server of ours. Like other app data, it can be included in your iPhone backup, depending on your settings.

Is there a subscription?

Yes. The only paid item is the monthly plan (¥200 a month, auto-renewing). The basics are free.

彼のトリセツ is an unofficial reading based on 16 types. It describes common tendencies as a rough guide, and does not decide his feelings or your future together.
