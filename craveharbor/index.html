Questions

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for or log in to.

What happens if I slip?

A slip is logged. Your quit date and your best streak stay. Your current streak counts from the slip, and your estimates are adjusted.

Where is my data stored?

On your iPhone, protected by its built-in data protection. It's included in your own iCloud or computer backups, and you can export a backup file anytime. It is never sent to us.

What's free, and what does Plus add?

“Help me through this moment”, the +1 counters, your own daily limit, 7 days of history, widgets and backups are always free. Plus adds more situation and trigger plans, Day 2 to Day 30 of the program, 30-day history, the full trigger ranking and danger hours.

Is Crave Harbor a medical treatment?

No. It's a tracking and self-help tool. For support with quitting, talk to a doctor or call 1-800-QUIT-NOW.
