Frequently asked questions

Which iPhones can record front and back at once?

Front+Back needs an iPhone XS, XR or later. On other iPhones TakeBoth records one camera and still saves the vertical and wide videos.

Where are my videos?

In the Photos app, as soon as you stop recording. Each view is its own video.

How do I restore Pro?

Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription, so there is nothing to cancel. On a new iPhone, tap Restore Purchases on the plans screen with the same Apple Account. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The phone got hot during a long take.

TakeBoth lowers the frame rate when the iPhone gets hot and keeps saving the take.
