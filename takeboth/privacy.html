Privacy Policy

TakeBoth records video on your iPhone and saves it to your Photos. We don't collect it, see it or sell it.

Effective October 30, 2026 · Velkmoor

What the app uses

  • Camera and microphone: to record video and sound while you record. If you deny the microphone, TakeBoth still records video without sound.
  • Photos (add only): to save the videos and photos you take. TakeBoth cannot read your library.
  • Settings and your script: kept in the app's preferences on your iPhone.

What we collect

Nothing. TakeBoth has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no third-party SDKs. Your videos never leave your iPhone unless you share them yourself.

If you turn on Share With App Developers in your iPhone's Settings, Apple may share anonymous crash reports with us. You control that setting.

Purchases

TakeBoth Pro is sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, email or payment details. The app only asks Apple's StoreKit whether Pro is active.

Deleting your data

Delete videos in the Photos app. Delete the TakeBoth app to remove its settings. We hold none of your data.
